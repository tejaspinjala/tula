# The game is the client: the Guild Master signs in to a world, not a dashboard; Phaser 4 stays, MonoGame is out

At the Guild Master's direction (2026-10-20), Guildhall is played, not browsed. Sign-in is a title screen. Signing in drops the Guild Master straight into the world: the Guild Hall, the Archive (library), the Quest Board plaza, the Treasury vault and the trail to the Wilds. Every other screen (Quest Board, Seals, Treasury, Ledger of Deeds, builders, Missives, settings) opens from a **Bag** menu as an in-game overlay. Members are never idle props. Where a Member stands and what it does shows its real work: reading the Archive walks it to the library, research sends it out along the trail to the Wilds, awaiting a Seal puts it at the Seal desk, and a Missive is a speech bubble and a line in the guild chat. Clicking a Member inspects it: its Quest, its live activity, its Missives, and a conversation with it.

**Engine: Phaser 4, not MonoGame.** MonoGame (C#/.NET, the engine behind Stardew Valley) would mean a second language and a duplicated contract layer for the shared Zod schemas. Its browser target is experimental, so the phone PWA and the tunnel would go. The Stardew look comes from the art and its rules (16px tiles, integer scale, layered Tiled maps), and Phaser renders those natively in the browser under our CSP (ADR-0009). An installable app is a thin **Tauri** shell around the same build: one codebase for web, PWA and desktop.

**Activity is a fact; behaviour is code.** The world reads what Members do from the event stream. A new ids-only `member.activity` event is emitted by guildd, never by the model, when a Member calls an MCP tool or a model turn starts. A pure client function maps each activity to a place and an animation. A model never decides where a character walks. Ambient behaviour while no Quest is held (wandering, browsing the Archive, chatting) is a deterministic schedule with templated lines. Model-written ambient chatter would spend the Treasury for decoration, so it is out unless a later ADR prices and caps it.

**Research is a governed tool.** Members get web research only through a guildd-side tool (search and fetch run by guildd, not the sandbox). The tool has a per-Quest cap, a Treasury charge, private-routing rules and results wrapped as untrusted data. The egress allowlist stays closed.

This supersedes ADR-0009's assumption that the React ledger is the primary surface. React remains for the overlay panels, built from the `pixel-ui` kit.
