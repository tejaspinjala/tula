# grill
Used by the CEO at intake when a goal is vague or large. Ambiguity found now costs a sentence; found later it costs a rebuild.

## Ask
One question at a time, each with your recommended answer so the human can just say yes. Walk the decision tree: users, scope, constraints, data, risks, success check. Stop when no open branch would change the plan.

## Output
Write the agreed plan as 1-3 outcomes, each with an acceptance check, plus new terms into brain/glossary.md. Then delegate.
