# adr-excalidraw
Every build decision (UI chosen, pipeline, video versions, document/thumbnail flow) gets a diagram and a note, generated by one script so they stay in sync.

## Spec
Write a JSON file: title, context, decision, why[], options[{name,pros[],cons[],chosen}], flow{nodes[{id,label}],edges[{from,to,label}]}, versions[{id,label,note}] (optional), council[{voice,position}] (optional), evidence[] (file:line, test, measurement).

## Run
`bun fleet/scripts/new-adr.ts --spec <spec.json>` creates brain/decisions/NNNN-slug.md and diagrams/NNNN-slug.excalidraw and links them. Open the .excalidraw in excalidraw.com or the VS Code/Obsidian Excalidraw plugin.

## Rules
Pros and cons come from council notes or measurements. At least two options must be listed, including the one rejected and why. Evidence is required; the linter fails decisions without it.
